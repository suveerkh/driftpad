# Driftpad

**Floating notes for your Mac that fade away unless you pin them.**

[**⬇ Download the latest version**](https://github.com/suveerkh/driftpad/releases/latest)

## Features

- **One click to a new note** from the menu bar icon
- **Floats above everything**: all apps, every desktop, full-screen apps
- **Disappears on its own** after 1 minute to 1 hour (default 2 minutes)
- **Pin** any note to keep it until you unpin it
- **Stays out of the way**: notes turn translucent after 2 seconds; hover or click to bring them back
- **Countdown pauses** while you hover over a note or type in it
- **Grows as you write**: a new note is one line tall and expands with your text
- **Liquid Glass design** with 7 preset colors or any custom color
- **Drag notes** anywhere on screen
- **Private by design**: no permissions, no network access, no tracking

Works on macOS 14 Sonoma or later, on Apple Silicon and Intel Macs.

## Install

Open the DMG and drag **Driftpad** into **Applications**.

On first launch, macOS will say it can't verify the app. Click **Done**, then go to **System Settings → Privacy & Security** and click **Open Anyway**. You only need to do this once.
